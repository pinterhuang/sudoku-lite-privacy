隱私權政策(繁體中文)

最後更新日期:2026 年 11 月 1 日

本隱私權政策適用於 Android 應用程式「Sudoku Lite」(套件名稱 tw.idv.pinter.sudoku.lite,以下稱「本應用程式」)。本應用程式由 阪誠網通(以下稱「我們」)提供。

1. 我們不收集的資料

本應用程式不要求註冊帳戶,不會要求或收集您的姓名、電子郵件、電話、精確位置、通訊錄、照片或其他個人身分資料。我們沒有自己的伺服器儲存您的資料。

2. 儲存在您裝置上的資料

遊戲進度與設定僅儲存在您的裝置本機資料庫,不會上傳。解除安裝本應用程式後,這些資料會一併刪除。

3. 第三方廣告服務(Google AdMob)

本應用程式使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。AdMob 可能自動收集並使用下列資料,用於投放與衡量廣告、分析及防止詐騙:

  • 裝置 ID 或其他 ID(例如廣告 ID)
  • 大概位置(依 IP 位址推算)
  • 應用程式當機記錄與診斷資料

本應用程式未開啟個人化廣告。您可以隨時在 Android 系統「設定 → Google → 廣告」中重設或刪除廣告 ID,或選擇停用廣告個人化。關於 Google 如何處理資料,請參閱:https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. 資料傳輸與安全

上述資料在傳輸時均透過加密連線傳送。

5. 兒童隱私

本應用程式並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計,我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

6. 資料刪除

我們不在自有伺服器保存您的個人資料,因此沒有可供刪除的帳戶資料。若要清除本機資料,請在系統設定中清除應用程式儲存空間或解除安裝。廣告相關資料請依第 3 節的方式於 Google 帳戶中管理。

7. 政策變更

我們可能更新本政策,更新後會修改上方日期,並公布於本頁面。

8. 聯絡我們

若對本政策有任何疑問,請來信:pinter.tw@gmail.com

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app "Sudoku Lite" (package tw.idv.pinter.sudoku.lite, the "App"), provided by 阪誠網通 (Bancheng Network) ("we").

1. Data we do not collect

The App requires no account and does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal identifiers. We do not operate servers that store your data.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device and are never uploaded. They are removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Third-party advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may automatically collect and use the following data to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and for fraud prevention:

  • Device or other IDs (such as the advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

The App does not enable personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads. For how Google handles data, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Security

The data described above is transmitted over encrypted connections.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers, so there is no account data to delete. To clear local data, clear the App's storage in system settings or uninstall it. Advertising-related data can be managed in your Google account as described in Section 3.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: pinter.tw@gmail.com
